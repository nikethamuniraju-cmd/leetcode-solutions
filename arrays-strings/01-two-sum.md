## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

We check every pair of elements in the array and compare their sum with the target value. If the sum equals the target, we print the indices of the two elements.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution should also handle cases where no pair produces the target sum.
